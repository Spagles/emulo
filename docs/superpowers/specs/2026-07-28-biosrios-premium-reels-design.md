# BiosRios Premium Reels Production Design

Date: 2026-07-28

Status: Approved direction, pending written-spec review

Owner: Ohad / BiosRios
Primary platforms: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts

## 1. Objective

Create a repeatable premium short-form system that turns verified AI skills,
repositories, prompts, tools, and experiments into high-retention videos and
qualified comments.

Each Reel must succeed as both:

1. A polished piece of entertainment and education.
2. A resource funnel with a specific `Comment "WORD"` call to action.

The production target is the quality demonstrated by the strongest files in
`D:\ditto\videos\refnces\videos`, including clean typography, intentional sound
design, strong transitions, designed backgrounds, rapid proof delivery, and a
clear visual hierarchy.

## 3. Recommended creative approach

Use a premium hybrid system.

- Use Ohad's real face when the source footage is sharp, naturally framed, and
  compatible with the spoken moment.
- Do not expose obvious lip-sync mismatch. Real face clips may be used as
  reactions, silent hooks, quick structural transitions, or native speech when
  the recorded line matches.
- Use the BiosRios character when real footage does not meet the quality or
  synchronization requirement.
- Generate original imagery only when it communicates a concept more clearly
  than a real screen, proof artifact, or existing character pose.
- Real product screens, repository excerpts, source documents, test receipts,
  and measured results remain the credibility layer.

This approach avoids dependence on HeyGen's exhausted Free quota. Future paid
avatar clips may replace selected host beats, but the Reel system must remain
publishable without them.

## 4. Reel structure

Default runtime: 35 to 50 seconds.

| Time | Function | Visual requirement |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0-1.2s | Contradiction or result | One dominant claim, number, or proof artifact |
| 1.2-4.0s | Trust and context | Real face or BiosRios character plus the exact subject |
| 4.0-20.0s | Proof | Real screens, source excerpts, or measured results |
| 20.0-35.0s | Mechanism | Step-by-step explanation with one visual question per beat |
| 35.0-43.0s | Reusable lesson | Clear conclusion that changes viewer behavior |
| Final 3-5s | Resource CTA | `Comment "WORD"` plus the exact resource received |

The result or contradiction must appear before introductory context. No logo
animation, greeting, generic setup, or delayed reveal is allowed.

## 5. Visual system

### Composition

- Format: 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- Protect the top 180 px and bottom 320 px from essential text.
- Keep primary evidence above the center line when platform controls could
  cover the lower-right area.
- Use full-screen face or face-plus-proof compositions, not a permanent small
  webcam box.
- Change the visual idea every 1 to 2.5 seconds. Hold longer only when the
  viewer must read a real artifact.

### Typography

- Hook and verdict type: heavy grotesk such as Archivo Black or Montserrat.
- Evidence labels and source lines: Space Mono or JetBrains Mono.
- Captions: three to six words per phrase.
- Emphasize one word or number per beat using Claude orange, signal red, or
  verified green.
- Captions must be designed as part of the composition, not added as generic
  platform subtitles.

### Color and backgrounds

- Default brand field: warm off-white with a subtle technical grid.
- Evidence cards: near-black with high-contrast source text.
- Primary accent: Claude orange.
- Secondary accents: red for invalid or blocked states, green for verified
  results, and blue only for Codex-specific evidence.
- Background changes must follow the story. Use soft gradients, restrained
  texture, cropped interfaces, and proof artifacts instead of decorative
  stock footage.

### Transitions and motion

- Prefer match cuts, card pushes, masked reveals, snap zooms, split-screen
  assemblies, number count-ups, and evidence highlights.
- Use motion to transfer attention from the claim to the proof.
- Avoid repeated white flashes, constant zooms, random spins, template
  transitions, and effects that reduce readability.
- Every animation must be deterministic and seek-safe in HyperFrames.

## 6. Sound design

Every Reel receives:

- One restrained background music bed that supports pace without competing
  with the cloned voice.
- Short interface clicks for verified selections and checklist states.
- Low impacts for major numbers and verdicts.
- Controlled whooshes for card or screen movement.
- One riser into the central reversal or strongest proof.
- A short signature hit for the final keyword CTA.

Sound effects must follow visible action. They may not be added continuously
for artificial energy.

Mix targets:

- Speech remains the dominant layer.
- Background music ducks under narration.
- Effects never obscure consonants or numerical claims.
- Final true peak stays at or below -1 dBTP.
- Review loudness against a phone speaker before approval.

## 7. Face, character, and generated-image rules

Priority order:

1. Real Ohad footage with correct visual quality and natural timing.
2. Approved BiosRios character pose matching the emotional beat.
3. Newly generated original image when a concept requires a visual that does
   not exist in the approved library.

Generated visuals must preserve BiosRios identity and the selected editorial
style. They may support a metaphor, reaction, or environment, but may not
replace real proof or depict a fake software interface as evidence.

Reject:

- Soft, distorted, or poorly cropped real-face footage.
- Visible speech that does not match the narration.
- Generic AI robots, neon cyberpunk imagery, and unrelated cinematic B-roll.
- Character poses that cover the proof.
- Generated screenshots presented as real product output.

## 8. Comment-resource funnel

Each Reel has one unique uppercase keyword.

The final CTA uses this format:

`Comment "WORD" and I’ll send you the [specific resource].`

Rules:

- The resource must already exist before the Reel is approved.
- The keyword must be easy to spell and unique within the active batch.
- The CTA appears only after the viewer receives a complete useful lesson.
- The first caption line repeats the CTA.
- The pinned comment identifies the resource and any important limitation.
- Publish materials include the destination URL or local handoff path, but no
  automated messaging or posting occurs without separate authorization.

## 9. Expanded reference research

The reference corpus grows continuously, but every entry must include useful
evidence rather than an unattributed download.

For each reference Reel, record:

- Creator and source URL when available.
- Public view count and observation date.
- Hook wording and first-frame composition.
- Face, character, screen, and proof usage.
- Caption style and average phrase length.
- Cut rhythm and transition families.
- Sound-design pattern.
- Comment keyword and promised resource.
- What BiosRios should transfer.
- What BiosRios should not copy.

Research must favor AI teaching, Claude Code, Codex, agent workflows, developer
tools, skills, repositories, and useful automation. Trends may inspire the
editing grammar, but claims and resources must come from primary sources.

## 10. Media workflow

All adopted media must be frozen into each HyperFrames project's media ledger.

- Reuse approved local assets before sourcing new media.
- Inspect every real video at early, middle, and late timestamps.
- Keep cloned voice and video picture tracks separate.
- Use real logos from official sources.
- Resolve music and effects through the project's media workflow.
- Apply photographic treatments only after inspecting the source and
  previewing the result.
- Preserve source attribution for every proof artifact.

## 11. Verification

Each Short must pass:

1. HyperFrames lint, runtime, layout, motion, and contrast checks with zero
   errors.
2. Snapshot review at every hook, face entrance, proof reveal, transition,
   conclusion, and CTA.
3. Full moving-preview review for pacing, lip-sync exposure, transition
   continuity, and sound synchronization.
4. Caption-safe-zone review for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
5. Claim review against the exact source.
6. Resource review confirming the promised link or file exists.
7. Final phone-speaker audio review.

Rendering remains user-gated. A final MP4 is produced only after Ohad approves
the refreshed visual and audio preview.

## 12. Failure handling

- If real-face footage fails quality or synchronization review, use the
  BiosRios character for that beat.
- If a claim lacks primary evidence, remove or weaken the claim before editing.
- If a resource link is unavailable, the Reel cannot use a comment-delivery
  promise.
- If generated imagery weakens identity consistency, use an approved character
  asset instead.
- If a transition creates a blank or washed-out frame, replace it with a
  direct cut, masked reveal, or bounded card movement.

## 13. Success metrics

Evaluate each Reel using:

- Three-second hold rate.
- Average watch time.
- Completion and replay rate.
- Comments per 1,000 views.
- Resource-keyword comments as a percentage of total comments.
- Shares and saves.
- Profile visits.
- Follows attributed to the Reel.

The first batch establishes the baseline. Future edits should change one major
hook or packaging variable at a time so results remain interpretable.

## 14. Acceptance criteria

The design is complete when:

- All three existing benchmark Shorts use the premium hybrid visual system.
- Each Short has polished sound design and no washed-out transition frames.
- Every hook proves its promise within the first five seconds.
- Real face, character, and generated images follow the priority rules.
- Each Short has one verified comment resource, keyword, caption, and pinned
  comment.
- The expanded reference audit records source and performance evidence where
  available.
- All project checks pass.
- Ohad approves the previews before rendering.

## 15. Out of scope

- Purchasing or upgrading HeyGen.
- Posting to Instagram or YouTube.
- Automatically replying to comments or sending direct messages.
- Inventing performance metrics, resource demand, or benchmark outcomes.
- Rebuilding the long-form episode during this first implementation batch.
